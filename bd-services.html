The Wrong People

The Method

How an Account Goes from Cold to Conversation

01

Intelligence

We profile the target — leadership, recent moves, budget cycle, current vendors, pressure points. Sources: open data, LinkedIn, press, Tadawul filings, regulatory bodies.

02

Angle

We design your specific way in — the problem the buyer is already paying for that you solve better. Not a pitch. A reason for them to reply.

03

Path

Warm intros where they exist. Direct outreach where they don't. WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or majlis — whichever channel matches the buyer's habit.

04

Handoff

When the conversation is qualified, we hand it to you — with full context, the buyer's stated priorities, and a recommended next move.
