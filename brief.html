Common questions.

Why a vertical Brief instead of a general Saudi business newsletter?
Because generic intelligence is for analysts writing reports, not operators making decisions. A logistics founder does not need a wealth management note. A family office principal does not need last-mile unit economics. You pay for relevance, not volume.
Who writes the Brief?
Anas Elimam writes every issue. Founder of LEOMAX, working from Riyadh, building Hoolak in parallel. The intelligence is grounded in active deal flow, not desk research.
Can I subscribe to more than one vertical?
Yes. Two verticals: SAR 999 / month. Three or four: SAR 1,499 / month. Founding member pricing applies proportionally. Email anas@leomaxglobal.com to set up multi-vertical access.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel any time from your account. No questions, no exit interview. Founding member pricing is forfeited if cancelled and resubscribed later.
Refund policy?
First 30 days, full refund, no questions. After that, no partial month refunds. The Brief is built for the long game.
Is the Brief in English or Arabic?
English by default. Arabic-only edition for the Family Office vertical is coming Q3 2026. Subscribers will be notified when it ships.
How is this different from a free LinkedIn post?
LinkedIn posts are about visibility. The Brief is about depth. Specific situations, named players, actual numbers, decision frames. The kind of analysis that does not make sense to publish for free.
How do I pay?
Stripe checkout in SAR via credit card or Mada. Annual subscribers can also pay via bank transfer. Saudi VAT applies where required.
