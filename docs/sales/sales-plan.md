## THE 4 ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles)

Pick ICP based on the conversation. Match offer to ICP.

### ICP #1 — KSA SME Founders ("بيدفعون عشان نخش لعملاء كبار")
- Revenue: SAR 1M–20M annually
- Pain: Can't break into enterprise sales (STC, Aramco, government, banks)
- Best offer: **Account Brief (free) → Account Access Sprint (15K) → BD-as-a-Service (7.5K/mo + commission)**
- Where to find: LinkedIn (search by job title "CEO" + location "Riyadh" + 11-50 employees)
- Warm path: 2nd-degree LinkedIn, WhatsApp business groups

### ICP #2 — Saudi/GCC Startups Raising Pre-A or A
- Stage: Have a working MVP + first revenue + planning to raise
- Pain: Pitch deck weak, unit economics fuzzy, investor list unknown
- Best offer: **Investment Readiness Sprint (SAR 18K)**
- Where to find: LEAP attendees, Misk Foundation alumni, Wamda mentions, Magnitt KSA list
- Warm path: founder communities (you've already in some via KBC + Egypt/Sudan)

### ICP #3 — Saudi Family Offices + UHNW Individuals
- Profile: Active in private deals but no internal diligence team
- Pain: Getting flooded with pitches, can't filter signal from noise
- Best offer: **Investor Memo Subscription (12K/mo)**
- Where to find: 2nd-degree intros via investment bankers, lawyers, family-office advisors
- Warm path: this is the HARDEST cold but the highest LTV when warm

### ICP #4 — Sudanese/Egyptian Founders Entering KSA (UNIQUE TO YOU)
- Profile: Companies in Khartoum/Cairo wanting to expand to Riyadh/Jeddah
- Pain: Don't know KSA market, regulatory environment, customer behavior
- Best offer: **Account Access Sprint + Diagnostic combo (SAR 16,500)** OR **Full Transformation (22K) if they need branding/positioning too**
- Where to find: KBC, Sudanese Business Council in KSA, Egyptian Chamber of Commerce
- Warm path: **THIS IS YOUR HOME COURT** — start here Day 1

### PHASE 2 — Warm Network Activation (Days 15-30)

**Goal**: Convert your existing network into 10 discovery calls.

This is the FASTEST channel. You already know these people.

#### The 50 People List (Day 15)
List 50 people in your network who:
- Are founders/executives at relevant companies
- Are well-connected (could refer you)
- Have done business with you before
- Are in KBC, Egyptian Chamber, Sudanese expat groups

#### The Activation Sequence (Days 16-30)

For each person, do ONE of these (don't do all — pick what fits the relationship):

**Tier A — Direct ask (10 people you're closest to)**:
```
"يا [اسم], أنا الفترة دي في LEOMAX بشتغل مع شركات سعودية في 3 خدمات:
1) Account Access Sprint — نوصل لـ 10 شركات سعودية كبار في 4 أسابيع
2) Investment Readiness — تجهيز للجولة في 14 يوم
3) Diagnostic — جلسة استشارية 90 دقيقة لو محتار من أين تبدأ

هل في [شركة معينة] أو في حد في شبكتك تظن يستفيد؟ ابعتلي اسمه وأنا أتابع."
```

**Tier B — Soft ask (20 people you know well)**:
```
"يا [اسم], أطلقت leomaxglobal.com كـ resource hub لتطوير الأعمال في السعودية.
27 مقال + sample كامل للـ Investment Readiness Sprint مطبق على شركات سعودية حقيقية.
لو شفت فيه فايدة، أكون ممتن لو شاركتها في شبكتك."
```
(يجيب link خلفي + يخلي الناس تكتشف الموقع)

**Tier C — Just connect (20 people you know less well)**:
```
"يا [اسم], تابعك من بعيد. كنت محتاج رأيك في حاجة — أنا أبحث عن 5 شركات سعودية في قطاع [X] أعمل لهم Account Brief مجاناً عشان أختبر نسخة جديدة من البحث.
تعرف حد في القطاع ده يحب يجرّب؟"
```
(يحول الـ ask لـ "أنا أخدمك أنت" — psychology trick)

**Daily target Days 15-30**: 3-5 messages/day.
**Weekly expected outcome**: 10-15 responses, 3-5 discovery calls booked.
