## PART 4 — DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT (30 min)

Open your CRM during the call. Take notes by ear, not by typing every word.

### Minute 0-2 — Warm open
```
"Thanks for the time. Quick housekeeping: I usually run these as a conversation, not a presentation. 
I'll ask you 5-6 questions, you talk most of the time, and at the end we'll know whether LEOMAX makes sense for what you're trying to do. Cool?"
```

### Minute 2-7 — Situation
```
1. "Walk me through what you do — 60 seconds version, like you'd say at a dinner party."
2. "What stage are you at right now — revenue, team, customers?"
3. "What's the #1 thing you're trying to solve in the next 90 days?"
```

**Listen for**: revenue numbers · team size · pain point · timeline urgency

### Minute 7-15 — Pain + Cost
```
4. "If I told you the #1 thing magically gets solved tomorrow, what changes in your business?"
5. "What have you tried so far? What worked, what didn't?"
6. "What's the cost of NOT solving this in the next 6 months?" 
   (key question — this is where they admit the urgency)
```

**Listen for**: previous failed solutions · budget signals · emotional weight

### Minute 15-22 — Fit + Offer
```
7. "Based on what you're describing, I see 2 paths we could work together:
   [Offer A — match to their situation, e.g. Account Access Sprint at SAR 15K, 4 weeks]
   [Offer B — bigger or smaller depending on signal]
   
   Which feels closer to what you actually need?"

8. "What's your timeline to make a decision?"
```

### Minute 22-28 — Objections + Close
Common objections + responses:

**"It's expensive"**:
```
"Compared to what? — if it gets you to your goal in 4 weeks vs 4 months, the math works.
Also, we structure it as 2 milestones: SAR 7.5K to start, SAR 7.5K at delivery. So you only commit half upfront."
```

**"I need to think about it"**:
```
"Of course. Two things to consider while you think:
1) What's the cost of NOT doing it for the next 6 months — your earlier answer
2) Three slots in our current engagement window. Want me to hold one until [Day+5]?"
```

**"Let me check with my partner/co-founder"**:
```
"Smart. Want me to send a 1-page summary you can share? Or join you on a 10-min call together?"
```

**"We're going to build this in-house"**:
```
"Reasonable. Two questions:
1) Who specifically owns it on your team?
2) When does it ship?
If those don't have crisp answers, you're better off with us. If they do, you're right — do it in-house."
```

### Minute 28-30 — Close
```
"Here's what I'll do: send you a 1-page proposal with scope, timeline, pricing by [day]. 
If it looks right, we kick off [date]. If not, just tell me what to adjust.

Last question: how do you prefer to communicate going forward — email, WhatsApp, or Slack?"
```
