Common Questions

Honest Answers

When is launch, exactly?

Closed beta opens Q3 2026 (July–September) with 12 design partners in logistics. General availability follows once we've shipped through the first quarter of real customer feedback. We don't ship dates — we ship readiness.

Why logistics first?

It's the sector we know best and the one with the clearest decision-density per week. Once the model proves there, we expand horizontally — F&B, retail, and SaaS are next in the roadmap.

What if I'm not in logistics?

Join the waitlist anyway. We'll let you know when your sector enters beta. If your use case is close enough, we may also fit you as an exploratory partner.

Will this replace our team's analyst?

No. It replaces the spreadsheet that your analyst spends 70% of their time updating. Their job becomes interpreting the recommendations and pushing back on them — which is what you actually hired them for.

Where does the AI run? Is our data private?

Data stays in our Saudi-region storage (KSA-based servers). AI calls go to Anthropic Claude via a privacy-preserved layer. No customer data is used to train models. Audit log on every AI call.

What if I want something now, not Q3?

Look at our BD & Access or consulting engagements — the same intelligence layer, delivered as a service while the product is being built.
