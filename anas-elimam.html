Bio

The work, in one paragraph.

Dr. Anas Elimam is a business transformation strategist based in Riyadh. He is the founder of LEOMAX, a strategic consultancy that engineers operating systems for ventures across Saudi Arabia, the GCC, Egypt, and Sudan, and the founder of Hoolak, a Saudi logistics venture.

His work sits at the intersection of business development, AI infrastructure, brand positioning, and operational strategy. He advises founders, family offices, and operators on positioning, systems, and disciplined execution. He writes about strategy, systems, and the modern competitive landscape on the LEOMAX journal.

Modern companies no longer compete only through products. They compete through systems, speed, operational intelligence, brand perception, data infrastructure, and decision quality. The work he leads is built around those six axes.
