02

No Placement Conflict

For Founders Raising

The 7 Dimensions of Investment Readiness

Investors don't pass on companies. They pass on founders who don't know their own numbers. We close that gap.

01 · Unit Economics

CAC, LTV, payback period, gross margin - defensible, sourced, and stress-tested for the next 18 months.

02 · Cap Table

Clean cap table modeling, dilution scenarios across 3 round paths, ESOP planning.

03 · Financial Model

Bottoms-up 3-year model with sensitivity analysis on the 3 variables that actually move outcomes.

04 · Pitch Narrative

Story restructured around the GCC investor's question hierarchy - not Silicon Valley templates.

05 · Diligence Pack

Data room organized to anticipate questions, not bury them. Speed = signal in this market.

06 · Investor Mapping

List of 20-40 right-fit investors with warm-intro paths where they exist, cold sequences where they don't.

07 · Founder Readiness

The hardest one. Can you answer the question you don't want to be asked? We rehearse that.
