How It Works

Structured, Not Casual

Unlike Valeria's open chat, each advisor follows a domain-specific intake — qualifying questions that match how that function actually works.

01

Pick Your Specialist

Choose the advisor whose domain matches your question. If you're not sure, ask Valeria first — she'll point you to the right one.

02

Domain Intake

The advisor opens with 3–4 qualifying questions specific to their function. This isn't small talk — they need the context to give a real answer.

03

Working Advice

Substantive recommendations, frameworks, and next steps. Not "you should consider…" generic. Specific, actionable, and grounded in how LEOMAX delivers.

04

Escalate If Needed

If the question outgrows the chat, the advisor offers a path: book Dr. Anas, request a brief, or trigger a discovery call.
