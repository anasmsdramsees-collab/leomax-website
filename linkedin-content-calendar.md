## ACCELERATION TACTICS (optional, week 4+)

### A) Carousel posts (every 2 weeks)
Pick the most-engaged article. Convert into 6-8 slide carousel:
- Slide 1: Hook (same as LinkedIn post)
- Slides 2-6: One key point per slide
- Slide 7: "Read the full piece →"

Carousels get **3-5× the reach** of regular posts.

### B) Tag relevant Saudi voices
Tag 1-2 thought leaders per post when the content genuinely connects.
NEVER spam-tag. Quality only.

### C) Video version
Record a 60-90 second selfie video on the article's main point.
Post it on Tuesday alongside the article.
Vertical 9:16 ratio. LinkedIn rewards native video.

### D) Comment-bait closing
End some posts with: "What's your take? Curious." or
"If you disagree, tell me where I'm wrong — I'll update."
